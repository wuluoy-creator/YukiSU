# Material Symbols

The `ms_*.xml` Android vector assets use Google's Material Symbols Rounded family,
at the default 24 px optical size, weight 400. Navigation also includes filled
Home, Admin Panel Settings, Extension and Settings variants to distinguish the
selected destination without relying on color alone.

Source: https://fonts.google.com/icons

Additional symbols were obtained from Google's public SVG distribution:
`https://fonts.gstatic.com/s/i/short-term/release/materialsymbolsrounded/{name}/{default|fill1}/24px.svg`.
The SVG paths are preserved; a group translates the SVG's negative Y viewport
into the Android vector viewport. Directional symbols support auto-mirroring.
